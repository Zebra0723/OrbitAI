Connection

This page talks straight from your browser to the bridge on your Mac. Nothing about your assistant is stored here, and no request passes through this site.


    
On the Mac: orbit console --allow https://your-project.vercel.app
      then orbit console --bridge, which prints the token and stays running.

  





  
People

  
Orbit can tell voices apart. Record yours once and it greets
  you by name; record the people you live with and it knows them too. Everything
  on this page used to need a terminal.

  
Checking what is set up on your Mac…






  
Record a voice

  

    

      
      
    

    
Click Record, then speak for a few seconds — what you
    had for breakfast, what you are working on, anything. Orbit stays quiet and
    will not act on it. Do it two or three times on different days and it gets
    noticeably better at telling you from somebody who sounds like you.

    

  






  
Voices it knows

  

  

    
    
    
  

  

  
Check reads back how well somebody's recordings agree with
  each other. One that agrees with none of the others caught the wrong person,
  and it drags the average towards them — which is how a stranger ends up
  being recognised as you. Prune drops those.






  
Somebody you would rather it ignored

  

    

      
      
    

    
The voices worth banning are the ones with no name, so
    this keeps the last thing it heard, labels it, and refuses it from then on.
    It will not let you ban yourself.

    

  






  
Turning voices away

  

  

    
    
    
  

  

  
Leave this off until you have checked it recognises you
  — say something, then Score that voice. If it scores you below the
  threshold, turning people away turns you away. Say the bypass code out
  loud to wave a refused voice through for one conversation.
