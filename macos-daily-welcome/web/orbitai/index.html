A voice that actually
runs your Mac.

OrbitAI listens for its name, answers in your own cloned voice, and does the thing: sends the message, places the call, drafts the replies, hands work to Claude, drives the apps. It lives in the menu bar and needs nothing open.

Open the console Set it up

What it does

Wakes with you

The first time the Mac wakes each day it greets you by name and reads the briefing: reminders due, today's calendar, unread messages and mail, and whatever Claude finished overnight.

Holds a conversation

Say its name once. After that it keeps listening until you say thanks, remembers who you were just talking about, and stops mid-sentence if you tell it to.

Acts, with a hand on the brake

Reversible things happen on the word. Sends, calls, quits and anything Claude writes are read back and wait for a spoken yes.

Answers

Questions go to Claude and come back as a sentence or two. Point it at your screen and it will explain the error you are looking at.

Sounds like

Welcome back, Arjun. Good morning, sir. It's eight forty-two in the morning, Monday the first of September. Three reminders due today, one overdue. Two events on the calendar. Top of the list: call the bank. That one is overdue. Standing by.

Times, dates and counts are written out as words before they reach the voice - engines mangle raw digits in their own particular ways.
