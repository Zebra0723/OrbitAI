#### 📖 Finding Joy — Project Summary

**Premise:** A YA novel about Laleh Yousafi, a girl who grows up in Taliban-controlled Afghanistan, secretly learns tennis on a rooftop, escapes, and becomes a pro. Book 1 of a planned trilogy.

**Structure:** 37 chapters. Frame story.

- **Ch 1** — Pro Laleh, mid-match at Wimbledon Quarterfinal. Three-mystery hook: a medallion, the name "Farah" dropped without explanation, cliffhanger line: *"Miss Yousafi, they're waiting for you."* Opponent = Sinclair.
- **Ch 2–4** — Setup. Birth (May 6, 1986). Family. Taliban takes Kabul (Aug/Sept 1996, Laleh is 10).
- **Ch 5–18** — Family crisis, Padar's arrest, secret rooftop tennis begins (Ch 15), Farah trains her.
- **Ch 19–21** — The escape. Border crossing with Farah.
- **Ch 22–25** — New country, juniors, first WTA whisper, Aziza watching from afar.
- **Ch 26–31** — Pro climb. Brutal first-round loss. Roland Garros qualifying fail. Comeback.
- **Ch 32–37** — Wimbledon arc. Ch 34 picks up directly from Ch 1's last line — no match replay. Family reunion in tunnel. SF, F. She wins.

**POVs (one narrator per chapter):** Young Laleh, Pro Laleh, Aziza (mother), Mohammed (father), Ahmed (brother), Farah (cousin/coach), Fatima (best friend).

**Locked-in closing paragraph (Ch 37):**

> In Buddhism, they seek enlightenment. In Islam, they try to obtain salvation. In Hinduism, they work towards achieving unity with the divine. As for me, an Afghan girl who was searching for freedom, I was finding joy. And I found joy.

**The trilogy:**

- **Book 1: Finding Joy** — Laleh's story, ~37 chapters.
- **Book 2: Recovering Connection** — Fatima's POV, escape to reunion with Laleh, ~42 chapters.
- **Book 3: Home Stretch** — both at top, Laleh career-ending injury at Wimbledon final, ~43 chapters.

**Trilogy mechanics:**

- **Surname trick:** Laleh Yousafi, Fatima Zahra. Kids in Kabul never knew each other's last names — that's why Fatima's 15-year search fails. She searches "Laleh" + Afghanistan/Kabul/refugee/missing — never "Yousafi," never "tennis."
- **Reunion (Book 2 final match, Option A):** walk-out recognition (half-second freeze) → strict pro warm-up/coin toss → ONE mid-match half-smile across benches → long hug at the net post-match → locker room reveal: *"I never knew your last name."* / *"I never knew yours either."* → letter is read here.
- Friendship survives all 3 books — never break it.

**Craft principles to enforce in the editor:**

- Stretch scenes with sensory detail. Don't over-explain.
- Keep POVs distinct (voice cheat sheet exists in chapter plan PDF).
- Best lines so far: *"lacerated but loyal."*
- Writing happens on iPad/Google Docs (weekends) and notebooks (weekdays).

## RSS system

Each section has a curated list of RSS feeds.

Vortex pulls:

- title
- summary
- date
- image if available
- source
- link

Then turns them into beautiful cards.

## Thumbs up/down adaptation

Every card has:

- 👍 More like this
- 👎 Less like this
- ⭐ Save
- 🚫 Hide source/topic

Vortex learns:

- which sections you open most
- which sources you like
- which topics you dislike
- which competition types are worth showing
- whether you prefer biographies, writing, tennis, LEGO, engineering, etc.

So the feed gets smarter without needing APIs.
